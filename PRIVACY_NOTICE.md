## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is collected automatically by our hosting provider, Vercel, by its privacy-friendly analytics tools, and by third-party content you choose to load (see [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content)). This may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language settings, referring website, approximate location (such as country or city), and information about which pages you view and how you interact with them. This information does not directly identify you by name.

### Information you send us

If you contact us by email, WhatsApp, or Instagram, for example to book a performance, we receive whatever you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number, and event details.

You are not required to give us any personal information to use the Site. If you contact us, we need your contact details to reply.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, such as information about health, religion, or ethnic origin. Please do not send it to us.

## 2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION, AND ON WHAT LEGAL BASIS?

We use information only for the purposes below. Where the GDPR applies, each purpose relies on the legal basis shown.

| Purpose | Information | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Delivering the Site and keeping it secure**, for example preventing abuse and fixing errors | Technical request data, such as IP address and browser type | Our legitimate interest in operating a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Understanding how the Site is used** and how fast it loads, so we can improve it | Aggregated, cookieless analytics and performance data (see [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content)) | Our legitimate interest in improving the Site, using tools designed not to identify or track visitors (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Replying to booking requests** and arranging and performing a booking | What you send us, such as your name, contact details, and event details | Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing that contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Replying to other messages** | What you send us | Our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Keeping business records** for tax and accounting | Booking and payment details | Compliance with our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| **Protecting our legal rights** | Relevant messages and records | Our legitimate interest in establishing or defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Loading third-party players** (YouTube, Spotify) | Whatever the platform collects once its player loads | Your consent, given by clicking the player (Art. 6(1)(a)) |

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not make any decisions about you by automated means, including profiling.

## 3. COOKIES, ANALYTICS, AND EMBEDDED CONTENT

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

**Hosting.** The Site is a static website hosted by Vercel Inc. To deliver the Site and keep it secure, Vercel processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. The Site itself does not set any cookies or use similar technologies to store information on your device. For details, see the [Vercel Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**Fonts and images.** All fonts and images are served from the Site itself. No information is sent to font or image services such as Google Fonts when you load a page.

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, for example which pages are viewed, the referring website, the country or city, and the browser and device type. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal identifiers that track you across websites. Visitors are counted using a hash of the incoming request, and that session information is discarded automatically after 24 hours. We only see aggregated statistics. For details, see [Vercel Web Analytics privacy and compliance](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

**Performance measurement.** We use Vercel Speed Insights to measure how fast pages load for visitors. On each page load it records anonymous performance metrics (Core Web Vitals) together with the page address, network speed, browser, device type, operating system, and country. It does not use cookies and is not tied to any individual visitor or IP address. For details, see [Vercel Speed Insights privacy and compliance](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/privacy-policy).

**Embedded players.** The Site offers DJ sets from **YouTube** and music from **Spotify**. These players are not loaded when you open a page: we show a preview image, and the player loads only when you click it, so nothing is sent to YouTube or Spotify until you choose to play. YouTube videos load in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

Once you load a player, that platform may set its own cookies and collect information about you, just as if you visited its website directly. The same applies when you follow a link to another website, such as Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Apple Music, TIDAL, or a ticket seller. This is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one. If you are logged in to one of these platforms, it may link your visit to your account there. See the [Google (YouTube) Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Spotify Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

**Managing cookies.** Because the Site itself sets no cookies and loads third-party players only when you click them, there is no cookie banner. Clicking a player is your choice to load it. You can withdraw that choice at any time by not loading players and by deleting or blocking their cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them will not prevent you from browsing the Site, but the players may not work.

If we add other third-party services to the Site in the future, we will update this notice first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before they load.

## 4. WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH?

We share information only in these situations:

- **Hosting.** Vercel Inc. hosts the Site and provides Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. It processes information on our behalf, under a data processing agreement.
- **Messaging providers.** When you contact us, your message passes through the service you choose: Google (Gmail) for email, or Meta (WhatsApp or Instagram) for messages. These providers process it under their own privacy policies.
- **Professional advisers.** Where needed, we may share booking records with our accountant or legal advisers, who are bound by confidentiality.
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or a government authority, or when needed to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## 5. IS YOUR INFORMATION TRANSFERRED INTERNATIONALLY?

We are based in Israel. The European Commission has recognized Israel as providing an adequate level of data protection, so information can flow from the European Economic Area to us without additional safeguards. The United Kingdom recognizes Israel in the same way.

Vercel, Google, and Meta process data on servers in the United States and other countries, and third-party players you choose to load may do the same. Where the GDPR applies, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

- **Hosting logs** are kept by Vercel for a limited period under its retention policy, for security and troubleshooting.
- **Analytics and performance data** is aggregated and kept according to Vercel's retention policy. Visitor session information is discarded after 24 hours.
- **Messages about a booking**, and the related booking records, are kept for as long as needed to handle the booking and afterwards for as long as Israeli tax and accounting law requires (generally 7 years).
- **Other messages** are deleted when they are no longer needed to handle your inquiry, normally within 2 years of our last contact.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it.

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, including under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and, for visitors in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- **correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **delete** your information;
- **restrict** how we use your information;
- **object** at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, including analytics;
- **receive** information you gave us in a portable, machine-readable format, or ask us to send it to someone else; and
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email [levi.ziv@gmail.com](mailto:levi.ziv@gmail.com) with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within one month. Making a request is free. Because we do not have user accounts, we may need you to give us enough detail (such as the email address you contacted us from) to find your information and confirm it is yours.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as Israel's Privacy Protection Authority, the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work, or the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first, so please consider contacting us before you do.
