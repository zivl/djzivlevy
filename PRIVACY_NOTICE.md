## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is collected automatically by our hosting provider, Vercel, by its privacy-friendly analytics tool, and by third-party content you choose to load (see [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content)). This may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language settings, referring website, approximate location (such as country or city), and information about which pages you view and how you interact with them. This information does not directly identify you by name.

### Information you send us

If you contact us by email or through social media, for example to book a performance, we receive whatever you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number, and event details.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, such as information about health, religion, or ethnic origin.

## 2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?

We use information only to:

- **Understand how the Site is used**, so we can improve its content and performance.
- **Reply to your messages** and handle booking or other inquiries you send us.
- **Keep the Site secure** and prevent misuse.
- **Comply with legal obligations** and protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. COOKIES, ANALYTICS, AND EMBEDDED CONTENT

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

**Hosting.** The Site is a static website hosted by Vercel Inc. To deliver the Site and keep it secure, Vercel processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. The Site itself does not set any cookies. For details, see the [Vercel Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, for example which pages are viewed, the referring website, the country or city, and the browser and device type. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal identifiers that track you across websites. Visitors are counted using a hash of the incoming request, and that session information is discarded automatically after 24 hours. We only see aggregated statistics. For details, see [Vercel Web Analytics privacy and compliance](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

**Embedded content.** The Site offers content from third-party platforms, such as music players and videos. These players are not loaded when you open a page: we show a preview image, and the third-party player loads only when you click it. Examples include:

- SoundCloud
- YouTube
- Spotify
- Mixcloud
- Instagram

Once you load a player, or when you follow a link to one of these platforms, that platform may set its own cookies and collect information about you, just as if you visited its website directly. This is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one. If you are logged in to one of these platforms, it may link your visit to your account there.

**Other services.** We may add or change embedded content, integrations, or other third-party services on the Site from time to time. Any such service may collect information and set cookies in the same way as described above, under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party service you interact with through the Site.

**Managing cookies.** Because the Site itself sets no cookies and loads third-party players only when you click them, there is no cookie banner. You can still view, block, or delete cookies set by third-party players in your browser settings. Blocking them will not prevent you from browsing the Site, but some players may not work.

## 4. WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH?

We share information only in these situations:

- **Service providers.** Vercel, our hosting provider, processes information on our behalf to deliver, secure, and measure the Site, including through Vercel Web Analytics.
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or a government authority, or when needed to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## 5. IS YOUR INFORMATION TRANSFERRED INTERNATIONALLY?

We are based in Israel. The Site is hosted by Vercel, which processes data on servers in the United States and other countries. Third-party players you choose to load may also process information in the United States and other countries. Those countries may not have data protection laws as comprehensive as those where you live. Where required, these providers use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

- **Analytics data** is aggregated and kept according to Vercel's retention policy. Visitor session information is discarded after 24 hours.
- **Emails and messages** you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting booking, and afterwards as required for tax, accounting, or legal purposes.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it.

## 7. CHILDREN

The Site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at [levi.ziv@gmail.com](mailto:levi.ziv@gmail.com) and we will delete it.

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, including under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and, for visitors in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, you may have the right to:

- ask for access to the personal information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct inaccurate information;
- ask us to delete your information;
- object to or restrict certain processing; and
- withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent.

To make a request, email [levi.ziv@gmail.com](mailto:levi.ziv@gmail.com) with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 30 days. Because we do not have user accounts, we may need you to give us enough detail (such as the email address you contacted us from) to find your information.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as Israel's Privacy Protection Authority or the authority in your country.

## 9. DO-NOT-TRACK SIGNALS

Some browsers offer a "Do-Not-Track" setting. There is no agreed standard for how websites should respond to it, so the Site does not currently respond to these signals. Our analytics do not use cookies or track you across websites in any case, and you can control third-party cookies through your browser settings as described in [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content).
