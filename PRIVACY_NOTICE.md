## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is collected automatically by our website platform, Wix, by its analytics tools, and by embedded third-party content (see [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content)). This may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language settings, referring website, approximate location (such as country or city), and information about which pages you view and how you interact with them. This information does not directly identify you by name.

### Information you send us

If you contact us by email or through social media, for example to book a performance, we receive whatever you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number, and event details.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, such as information about health, religion, or ethnic origin.

## 2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?

We use information only to:

- **Understand how the Site is used**, so we can improve its content and performance.
- **Reply to your messages** and handle booking or other inquiries you send us.
- **Keep the Site secure** and prevent misuse.
- **Comply with legal obligations** and protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. COOKIES, ANALYTICS, AND EMBEDDED CONTENT

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

**Wix.** The Site is built, hosted, and managed on the Wix platform, provided by Wix.com Ltd. Wix places cookies that are essential for the Site to work and to stay secure. With your consent, it also places analytics cookies, which help us understand how visitors use the Site through Wix's analytics tools and connected services such as Google Analytics. Wix handles these cookies and the related consent settings on our behalf. For details, see the [Wix Privacy Policy](https://www.wix.com/about/privacy) and Wix's explanation of [cookies used on Wix sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cookies-and-your-wix-site). You can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Embedded content.** The Site includes content embedded from third-party platforms, such as music players, videos, and social media feeds. Examples include:

- SoundCloud
- YouTube
- Spotify
- Mixcloud
- Instagram

When a page with embedded content loads, or when you play or interact with it, that platform may set its own cookies and collect information about you, just as if you visited its website directly. This is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one. If you are logged in to one of these platforms, it may link your visit to your account there.

**Other services.** We may add or change embedded content, integrations, or other third-party services on the Site from time to time. Any such service may collect information and set cookies in the same way as described above, under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party service you interact with through the Site.

**Managing cookies.** You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the cookie banner on the Site. You can also view, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from browsing the Site, but some embedded players may not work.

## 4. WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH?

We share information only in these situations:

- **Service providers.** Wix, our website platform and hosting provider, and the analytics services connected through it (such as Google Analytics) process information on our behalf to run, secure, and measure the Site.
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or a government authority, or when needed to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## 5. IS YOUR INFORMATION TRANSFERRED INTERNATIONALLY?

We are based in Israel. The Site is hosted by Wix, which stores and processes data on servers in the United States and other countries. Analytics and embedded content providers may also process information in the United States and other countries. Those countries may not have data protection laws as comprehensive as those where you live. Where required, these providers use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

- **Analytics and cookie data** is kept according to the retention policies of Wix and the analytics services connected through it.
- **Emails and messages** you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting booking, and afterwards as required for tax, accounting, or legal purposes.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it.

## 7. CHILDREN

The Site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at [levi.ziv@gmail.com](mailto:levi.ziv@gmail.com) and we will delete it.

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, including under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and, for visitors in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, you may have the right to:

- ask for access to the personal information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct inaccurate information;
- ask us to delete your information;
- object to or restrict certain processing; and
- withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent.

To make a request, email [levi.ziv@gmail.com](mailto:levi.ziv@gmail.com) with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 30 days. Because we do not have user accounts, we may need you to give us enough detail (such as the email address you contacted us from) to find your information.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as Israel's Privacy Protection Authority or the authority in your country.

## 9. DO-NOT-TRACK SIGNALS

Some browsers offer a "Do-Not-Track" setting. There is no agreed standard for how websites should respond to it, so the Site does not currently respond to these signals. You can use the cookie banner, the Google Analytics opt-out, and your browser's cookie settings described in [section 3](#3-cookies-analytics-and-embedded-content) instead.
