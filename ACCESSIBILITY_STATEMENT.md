## 1. CONFORMANCE STATUS

The Site is designed to meet Israeli Standard **SI 5568** (accessibility guidelines for web content), which adopts the W3C **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)**, at **level AA**, as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013. We work to WCAG 2.1 level AA.

The Site is **partially conformant**: the pages we build meet level AA, but some content provided by third parties does not fully meet it (see [section 3](#3-known-limitations)).

## 2. ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES

- **Structure:** pages use standard HTML headings, landmarks (header, main content, footer, navigation) and lists, so screen readers can move around them. A "Skip to content" link is the first thing on every page.
- **Keyboard:** everything on the Site works with a keyboard alone, with a clearly visible focus indicator. The photo viewer can be closed with the Esc key, and focus returns to where you were.
- **Screen readers:** meaningful images have text descriptions, decorative images are hidden from assistive technology, icon-only links have text labels, and links that open in a new tab say so. Each page declares its language.
- **Text and color:** text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. Text follows your browser's font-size setting and can be zoomed to 200% or more. The layout adapts to small screens without horizontal scrolling.
- **Motion and sound:** nothing plays automatically. The YouTube and Spotify players load only when you choose to start them. If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are turned off.
- **Languages and formats:** the main pages are available in English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese. Each page also has a plain-text Markdown version (for example [/epk.md](https://djzivlevy.com/epk.md)), which some people find easier to read with assistive tools.
- **No overlay widgets:** we do not use an "accessibility toolbar" plugin. Accessibility is built into the Site's code, and it works with the accessibility settings of your own browser and operating system.

## 3. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- **Third-party players and websites.** Once you start them, the YouTube and Spotify players are run by those companies. Links to Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, Apple Music, TIDAL and ticket sellers take you to websites we do not control. We cannot guarantee that this third-party content is accessible.
- **DJ set videos.** The videos are recordings of continuous music with no speech, and they do not have captions or audio description. The title and length of each set are shown as text.
- **Legal pages.** The Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy and this statement are available in English only, with a Hebrew summary of this statement below.

If any of this prevents you from getting information you need, contact us (see [section 5](#5-feedback-and-accessibility-coordinator)) and we will provide it in another way.

## 4. HOW WE TEST

We check the Site with automated accessibility testing (axe-core), keyboard-only navigation, and browser zoom up to 200%, on desktop and mobile browsers. We check it again when we make significant changes.
